# Members UX and motion pass

The public dashboard separates Guest browsing, local initiative bookmarks, private workspace preferences and studio editing. Public studio forms are read-only previews; no new role or authorization is introduced. Existing API/auth/RLS and the data schema are unchanged.

Section URLs and initiative detail URLs work independently of component state. Detail links carry a guarded internal return path; closing replaces the detail history entry. Funding summaries and running balances derive from ledger entries before filters. Initiative cards do not represent separate crowdfunding campaigns. The private vote saves one personal demonstration preference and does not award funds or aggregate a community tally.

Public partner context lives in `src/lib/members/presentation.js`. Vanni Hope is confirmed for Microcredit by the public Aram initiative page. Other named partnerships, additional joint initiatives and specific districts must be confirmed before adding them. The AI learning concept is unapproved. Fictional ledger records are not receipts or verified impact evidence.

`AramBrand.jsx` keeps the exact district SVG and community photograph. The overview hero shows the original transparent logo through `AramIdent.jsx`. Nothing loads or sounds until the visitor presses Play; the resting state is the original `<img>`. Play rebuilds the logo on a canvas from its own pixels: the Tamil word is written along its strokes, the pulli is pressed, the figure rises from ம, the sun arrives with three rays, and the four i-dots land in the colours of the light with the same notes. The final frame matches the original logo. Controls are Play, Pause, Resume, Replay and a remembered Sound toggle; playback pauses when the tab is hidden, and reduced motion plays the same order as fades without movement. There are no captions or added text.

The ident is code, not footage. `scripts/aram-ident/build_timing.py` derives `public/members/ident/aram-ident-timing.png` and `identLayers.js` from the logo (and refuses a logo whose hash differs from the protected original). Cue sheets in `src/components/members/ident/cues/` set timing and motion for the signature and three export variants (sting, lively and pencil). `scripts/aram-ident/synth_audio.py` synthesises each soundtrack from its cue sheet; the pencil variant uses pencil-on-paper sounds instead of music. Transparent exports (VP9 alpha WebM, QuickTime Animation) are rendered from the same renderer and are not shipped in `public/`. The earlier OpenArt study remains excluded.

Member sections share one dashboard instance through `src/app/members/(dashboard)/layout.jsx`. Navigating no longer remounts the dashboard or refetches its data, so the sidebar indicator travels, and an initiative opened from a card keeps the originating section (with its filters) behind the panel. The card artwork travels into the panel and back on close, closing returns focus to the card, and save and share confirm in place. Unknown member paths still return 404 inside the dashboard shell.

Public thought leadership lives at `/research/insights` and community events at `/community/events`; both are also available in the dashboard and on its overview. `src/data/community.js` is the shared public content source. The featured article preserves its real authors, publication date and Substack destination. No event is invented: only announced, scheduled future events appear, with a clear empty state until any are confirmed. Event times include a named time zone.

`/p1` is a small comparison landing page with V1 and V2 buttons. `/p1/v1` preserves the pre-feedback guest dashboard from commit `aa8441803bffd4ba2cbf12c13bad832d28b7f005`. Its only intentional adaptations are isolated selectors/animation names, the same public fixture anonymisation as the original guest API, and replacement of live API calls with that frozen fictional fixture. It cannot read or edit a private workspace. V2 opens `/members`.

Integration review fixed three playback/panel issues: the decorative backdrop no longer intercepts dismissal, Sound keeps an in-memory preference when storage is unavailable, and pending ident starts are invalidated when the component leaves or the tab is hidden. Playback still starts only after Play. The lossless square masters for all four exported cuts retain the source logo's exact alpha and opaque RGB; low-alpha colour differences after export are premultiplication rounding below 1/255. The dashboard uses the Signature cut; the other exports are supplied separately.

Validate with `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` (48 tests), focused ESLint for members source/routes/tests, `npm run build`, and desktop/phone keyboard and visual checks. No production deployment is implied by these checks. The full owner email-link/session/save flow, historical private-workspace import, real iPhone playback and subjective sound quality remain separate verification tasks.
